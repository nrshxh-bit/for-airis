One More Thing...

There's something I've been wanting to tell you, and I don't think I've ever properly explained just how much you've changed my life.

Before you came into my life, I wasn't really the kind of person who smiled a lot. I kept to myself, I wasn't always the friendliest person, and honestly, there were days when I didn't even feel like smiling.

But then you came along.

And somehow, without even realising it, I started becoming a different version of myself.

I became happier. I started smiling more, laughing more, and genuinely looking forward to the little things in life.

You even gave me the confidence to post Instagram stories, which might sound silly, but if you knew how rarely I used to post anything, you'd understand how big of a deal that was for me.

Even my close friends and family noticed the difference. They could see that I was happier, that I smiled more, and that something about me had changed.

And that something was you.

You also made me want to do better in my studies. Every little word of encouragement you gave me meant more than you probably realised. Even the smallest bit of support from you could make my entire day better.

It felt like having someone in my corner, someone who believed in me.

And I can't explain how special that feeling was.

I don't think I've ever looked forward to someone's messages the way I look forward to yours. The random conversations, the late-night talks, the sleep calls... all of it.

Even sleep calling was never really something I did that often before. But with you, I actually looked forward to it.

Because it was you.

And you know why I'm telling you this?

Because I didn't even realise how we went from being complete strangers to casually sending each other our childhood pictures, like we'd known each other for years.

It's crazy when you think about it, right? We started off knowing absolutely nothing about each other, and somehow, we became this comfortable with one another without even noticing.

And honestly, I think that's one of the things that makes what we have so special to me. 🤎

Honestly, it's been such a long time since I've felt this kind of love and happiness.

That's why, when things started feeling different between us, I got scared.

When our conversations became shorter and the replies felt a little more distant, I started wondering if maybe you were losing interest in me, or if I'd done something wrong.

I missed how close we felt.

That's one of the things I want to do better.

And that's also why I made this little website for you.

Not because I think a website can magically fix everything, or because I expect you to forgive me after reading it.

I made it because there are so many things I've been wanting to tell you, and I wanted to put some genuine effort into showing you how much you mean to me.

I don't know if you still think about those plans the way I do, but if you ever do, I'd love to hear about it.

Because more than anything, I want us to be okay, even if things take time, and even if the future looks different from what we planned.

I'm willing to put in the effort to make things right, but I know that it takes more than just words, and I know it's not something I can decide on my own.

Whatever happens, I'll always be grateful that I got to know you.

Because you reminded me what it feels like to be excited about life, to look forward to tomorrow, and to feel genuinely happy about the smallest things.

And before I end this, there's one more thing I've been wanting to say.

I love you.

Yep, I said it.
